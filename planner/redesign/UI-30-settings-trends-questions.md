Where Display lives on desktop (per user, shown among household settings). The calories-by-day "near goal" margin. Favourites per member or household. What "Recomp · maintenance minus 5%" is backed by. Where Needs attention, possible duplicates and social readers go.

Decided 2026-11-08, following FigCraft:

- Display (Auto, Light, Dark) appears twice on desktop: its own page in the Settings menu, and a row in the Household card on the Settings home.
- "Near goal" is within 120 kcal (FigCraft's Trends legend).
- Favourites are per member.
- "Recomp" is a goal name the member writes, shown with the date the goals were set ("Since Oct 2 · maintenance minus 5%").
- Needs attention has no badge and no page: in place on the phone; a card in Today's right-hand column on desktop (count, one row and one action per item); a chat widget grouped by kind. Possible duplicates and social reader notices, which FigCraft does not show, go under Settings › Household.
- Editing on desktop opens a 480-px panel from the right over a dimmed page (FigCraft "Desktop · Today · edit a food"); confirmations use a centred dialog.
