# The household data model in detail

Read this before touching foods, fridge stock, meals with items, usuals, recipe step mentions, recipe drafts, week plan repeats or recipe versions per person (`src/domain/foods.ts`, `stock.ts`, `meals.ts`, `food-quantity.ts`, `unit-conversion.ts`, `usual-add.ts`, `linking.ts`, and their db adapters). The invariants in brief are in `CLAUDE.md` ("Household data model"); week plan repeats are described only here.

## Foods

Every pillar points at one household `foods` row (`src/domain/foods.ts`). Fridge items, grocery lines and recipe ingredients carry `food_id`. Dislikes are free text: `member_dislikes.food_id` exists but nothing sets it. Barcodes and aliases hang off a food (`food_barcodes`, `food_aliases`). Manual names resolve through `normalizeFoodName`, so "Eggs", "eggs " and "EGGS" are one food, and `mergeFoods` repoints every reference when duplicates slip in. Supplies are foods with `kind = 'supply'`.

### Nutrition basis

`foods` holds nutrition **per 100 g** (per 100 ml when `nutrition_basis = '100ml'`), and null means unknown, never zero. `catalogNutritionFromOff` writes OFF's `per_100`. A per-100 ml food scales by ml (`nutritionScaleAmount`): g or each need its `grams_per_ml`, never a guessed density, and no unit means ml. `serving_*` is the pack serving; `perServingFromFood` needs matching units.

### Product quality

The same row also stores OFF product-quality columns: Nutri-Score, NOVA, Green-Score A+–F (`ecoscore_grade` as `a-plus`…`f`), additive/allergen/trace/analysis/label tags, and `off_quality_fetched_at`.

- Null means unknown; `[]` means OFF listed none.
- `offQuality` null means never looked up; an all-null quality with a `fetchedAt` means OFF had nothing for that barcode.
- Additive risk tiers are derived on read from generated `src/domain/additive-risk.ts` (`bun scripts/gen-additive-risk.ts`).
- Quality writes go through `setFoodQuality`, which updates only those columns.

## `food_id` is the identity

`food_id` is `not null` on fridge items, grocery lines and recipe ingredients since the Phase 8 contract (`20261031180000`). There is no identity JSON and no per-ingredient nutrition: "already have" matching (`sameFood` in `src/domain/linking.ts`) compares `food_id` alone, and recipe macros read the food row.

## Units

Quantities keep their own unit. `toGrams` (`src/domain/food-quantity.ts`) is the one conversion path: volume needs `grams_per_ml`, `each` needs `grams_per_each`, both from `resolveFactors` (`unit-conversion.ts`). A missing factor is null, never a guess: the recipe is "incomplete", with the factor named.

## Food-backed meals

`meal_items` rows are write-time snapshots (`buildMealFromItems` in `src/domain/meals.ts`). When a meal has items, the write path sets the `meals` totals to the null-aware sum of the snapshots, so every reader of `meals` is unchanged. Editing a food later never rewrites a logged meal, and Again, copy yesterday and an amount edit rescale a stored item when the food cannot scale its unit. Free-text meals with no items still work exactly as before.

## Stock ledger

`fridge_items` is current state. `stock_movements` is the audit trail, written by `applyMovement` in `src/domain/stock.ts` for put-away, cook, eat and discard. Manual `add_fridge_item` / `update_fridge_item` / `delete_fridge_item` and the web forms behind them write an `adjust` movement through the same function (`addFridgeItemAdjust`, `updateFridgeItemQuantityAdjust`, `deleteFridgeItemAdjust`), so `ledgerMatchesStock` holds for every item.

- Decrements go oldest expiry first and never below zero: a shortfall is reported, not clamped.
- A decrement aimed at one `fridgeItemId` only touches that item.
- A movement never records the id of an item it just deleted: `fridge_item_id` is a foreign key, so it would be rejected.

## Usuals

A plain food is a usual when it has live products or `usual_added_at` is set; `usualProductRows` is the one rule.

- The stamp marks a usual with no product to make it one. Type it sets it, and so does adding a scanned or searched row that is itself the plain food.
- Filing a product under a food does not stamp it: the product is what makes it a usual, so removing the last product takes it off the list.
- Filing a stamped food under another (`linkProduct`, or a merge that leaves it a product) clears its stamp.
- Otherwise only `removeUsual` (and the bulk remove) clears it.

Add a usual (`src/domain/usual-add.ts`) has three ways in:

- **Scan** files the product under a plain food as its usual product through the Settings › Items "Belongs to" link path. A branded product never merges into a plain food by name; an unbranded hit named like a plain food is that food.
- **Search** adds a food as it is.
- **Type it** needs calories, protein, carbs and fat per 100 g, per 100 ml or per a g-or-ml serving, and keeps a barcode that found nothing.

## Step mentions

Imported recipes only. `findMissingMentions` is pure: reviewed bundled words plus catalog names and aliases, with synonyms, kinds and parts covered, water and ice never flagged, and a miss preferred to a false alarm.

- Warnings are computed on read, never a `RecipeProblem`, so they never block verification, cooking, planning or groceries.
- Dismissals live in `recipe_imports.dismissed_mentions`, appended in place (`addDismissedMention`), so re-checks and import-row rewrites keep them.
- Add uses the step's amount, else one pack serving, else 1 each, with the step's descriptive words as the note.
- MCP returns `mention_warnings` (there is no dismiss tool).

## Week plan repeats

A repeating plan is one `plan_rules` row (`src/domain/plan.ts`, `src/db/plans.ts`): a recipe, meal and servings from `starts_on`, as once, every week, every day, weekdays or picked days (`days`, 0 = Sunday). One-off plans stay `recipe_plans` rows.

- `PlansStore.listPlans` expands the repeats into dated entries with ids `rule:<rule id>:<yyyy-mm-dd>`, so every plan reader (the week view, Today, groceries' missing-for-plan, the plan notices) sees them. Nothing else reads the two tables.
- Days are whole calendar days, so a daylight saving change cannot drop or double one.
- One entry per recipe, day and meal, never merged. Planning a recipe on a day and meal it already holds (a one-off or a day of a repeat) is already planned: nothing is written, whatever the servings, and the person changes the servings on the entry that is there. A different recipe, or the same recipe at another meal, is a plan of its own. A move onto a slot that already holds the same recipe and meal is the same answer (`PlanMove.alreadyPlanned`) and changes nothing.
- A repeat that would show on a day another repeat of the same recipe and meal shows on is refused (`PlanRepeatOverlapError`, which names the weekdays and how the other repeat repeats); asking again for a repeat that already runs is already planned. A repeat created over one-offs skips those days and leaves the one-offs and their servings alone. Nothing sums servings, on write or on read: `planEntries` is the one-offs and the expanded days in day, meal and planning order, so a plan entry names at most one repeat (stored data that somehow held a day twice would list both).
- Removing one day adds it to `skipped_on`, in that repeat only. Removing the whole series sets `ends_on` to the day before. Moving a day onto a free slot, or changing its servings, skips it and writes a one-off, in one transaction; saving a day with nothing changed writes nothing.
- Every write runs under the household plan lock. `plan_rules` is in `AGENT_TABLES`, so an agent's series removal is undoable.

## Recipe versions per person

A recipe has one base. Each member may override single ingredients of it, and their version is the base with their rows applied (`src/domain/recipe-variants.ts`; rows in `recipe_variant_overrides`, `user_id` being the member, as in `recipe_portions`). Nothing copies the base.

- A row says what it does to one base ingredient: `remove`, `amount` (amount and unit; no amount is "to taste"), `food` (another food, optionally with an amount; no amount and no unit reads the base's, no amount with a unit is "to taste") or `add` (an ingredient only this person has, `ingredient_id` null). One row per person and base ingredient: setting it again replaces it, and an `amount` on an ingredient the person swapped updates the swap's amount (or "to taste") instead of dropping the swap. `add` and `food` take a food only: a supply (`kind = 'supply'`) is refused with the base's "That catalog item is a supply." An `add` is one row per person, recipe and added food (unique index `recipe_variant_overrides_added_food_key`), so a retried add replaces itself. An amount on a `food` row fixes its unit when it is saved (the base's unit then), so a later edit of the base's unit does not rescale it.
- `variantFor(recipe, overrides, userId)` is pure. A base edit flows into every version except at an ingredient that version overrides. Deleting a base ingredient takes its overrides with it (a cascade), and one left behind is ignored, so a removed ingredient never comes back. Macros per version go through `macrosForPerson` on the version's ingredients; `variantCoverage` says who has a version.
- `recipeNeeds` totals what a cook, a grocery add or a plan needs when some portions are people's: each member's portions use that member's version, and any portions beyond the members' (cook and the grocery add take arbitrary servings) count at the base. The total is never below the members' sum. Lines of one food in units that convert (300 g and 0.3 kg; with `foodsById`, also g and ml through the food's density) are one line, so stock is netted once and grocery, cook and plan agree. Member ids match whatever their case (`sameMember`); the tools store the lower-case form. The lines already hold the totals, so the code that scales by `total / yield` takes them with yield and total the same.
- Readers: `cookRecipe` and `previewCookRecipe` (`memberPortions`, `variants`), `addRecipeToGrocery` (`members`, `variants`), `planRecipesOf` and `missingForPlan` (a plan's stored `member_ids`, each at their saved portions; a plan that names people is exactly those people: its count is the sum of their current saved portions, one for a person with none (`countedServings`), at each one's own version, with no leftover servings at the base, so a person who lowers their portions lowers the plan with it; `planRecipe` stores that sum as `servings` when it writes the plan (`defaultPlanServings`) and ignores a `servings` given with `member_ids`, `movePlannedRecipe` refuses one ("This plan is for named people; change their portions instead.") and the edit sheet shows the count instead of a stepper; a plan that names nobody counts its servings at the base), `resolveAndBuildMeal` (`forUserId`, `variants`: a logged portion is worked out from the person's version) and `getRecipeView`. Without members or without the store they read the base, but still through `recipeNeeds`, so a food the recipe lists twice is one line whoever has a version.
- `recipe_plans.member_ids` and `plan_rules.member_ids` hold who a plan is for (empty: nobody named). A day of a repeat and a one-off moved off it carry them.
- MCP: `get_recipe`'s `member_id` selects that member's version (a read of any member). `set_recipe_variant` and `clear_recipe_variant` act only for the person the agent acts for (`requireActingMemberId`); clear goes through `runDestructive` with `{ target }` and `recipe_variant_overrides` is in `AGENT_TABLES`.
- One row per person and ingredient is merged in the store under a lock (`mergeWithEarlier`): an `amount` on a swapped ingredient keeps the swap, a swap with no amount on an ingredient the person already gave an amount (an `amount` row or an earlier swap), or "to taste", keeps it, and an `amount` with no unit on a base ingredient that has none is refused.
- Merging foods folds a person's two `add` rows (the dropped food and the kept one on one recipe) into the kept food's row, amounts summed when they convert (`foldAddedAmounts`); the recorded merge deletes the dropped row, so its undo brings both back. The plan allergen notice checks each person a plan names against their own version; a plan that names nobody checks the base for everyone. Nobody else eats a plan that names people, so an unnamed member is not warned about it.
- Compare and align (admin and owner, `household.align_recipe_versions`; app only, no MCP tool). `compareVersions` is the compare view: the base lines, each member's differences (removed, amount, swapped, added, each with the `ref` that names it and the `rowKey`, one `id@updatedAt`, of the stored row it shows) and who uses the base; `GET /recipes/:id/versions` serves it as JSON. `alignChange` pushes one person's amount, swap or added line into the base for everyone, in one transaction (`recipeAlignTransaction`). `POST /recipes/:id/versions/align` posts the `ref` as `ingredient_id` and the compare read's `rowKey` as `row_key`; align refuses with "Their change changed; look again." when that row was saved again since, so what the admin saw is what is pushed: the person's own row goes, so does any other person's row on that ingredient that now says what the base says (units that convert count as equal), and a row that still differs stays. Another person's added line of the same food with a different amount becomes an amount on the new base line, so they never get the food twice. Everyone else keeps cooking exactly what they cooked: for each other person with a row on that ingredient, align works out the food and amount their row gave them over the old base, drops the row only when that is what the new base says with no row at all, and otherwise rewrites it so it still says that (Bob's "3 each" stays eggs after a swap, an amount equal to the swap's becomes a swap back to the old food rather than a dropped row, and a swap that followed the base amount is pinned to the old amount). A removal of that ingredient stays. The base edit goes through the recipe edit functions (`addRecipeIngredientById`, `swapRecipeIngredientFood`, `updateRecipeIngredient`). Align holds the recipe's `recipe-align:<lower-case id>` advisory lock exclusively and every write to a version (`saveOverride`, `deleteOverride`, `clearVariant`, `lockVersions`, and deleting the recipe) holds it shared, so a save in flight is either read or waits; `clear_recipe_variant` takes it (`lockVersions`) before it records the rows it deletes. Align locks the base ingredient row (`lockBaseIngredient`) before it reads anything, so a base edit that commits while it waits is what it reads, pins the others against and checks the verified recipe on, and before it touches any override, the order removing an ingredient and merging foods already use, so none of them deadlock; each row align removes goes through `deleteOverrideIfUnchanged` and the align stops, changing nothing, when a row is no longer what it read. A removal is refused (take the ingredient out of the recipe itself), and a saved recipe is not allowed to fail its check because of the change (`assertEditKeepsCheck`, before the person's row is dropped). `resetVariant` takes back the rows of one person that the admin was shown: `POST /recipes/:id/versions/reset` changes nothing until `confirm=1` and answers with what would go (`rowKeys`, one `id@updatedAt` per row; the compare read and that preview answer errors as `{ error }` JSON), and the confirm posts them back as `row_keys`. It deletes exactly those rows, in one transaction, and refuses with "Their version changed; look again." when the person's rows are no longer exactly that set (one added or saved again since); the confirmed post answers with the recipe page. Neither goes through `runDestructive`: a web route has no agent context.
- The app's recipe page shows one version at a time: the viewer's own by default, another member's or the base through its whose-version pull-down (`?member=`), and admins and the owner reach the compare view at `GET /recipes/:id/compare`. Its Cook takes the amounts of the version shown off the kitchen (the cook sheet posts it as `version`; a sheet that names nobody, or a non-member, cooks the base) and the page it returns to keeps that `?member=`; the portion it logs for the viewer is worked out from the viewer's own version (the base when they have none), as `cook_recipe` logs each person from their own. Align and reset posted from the compare page (`back=compare`) land on it, and a refusal shows there. The log sheet still logs a recipe portion from the base.

## Recipe drafts

An imported recipe is a draft until a person saves it: a draft is a `recipes` row whose `verification` is `unverified` (`isRecipeDraft`); there is no status column. Every import (`import_recipe_from_url`, `import_recipe_from_text`, the app's import) writes it with `draft_expires_at` = import time + 7 days. Hand-made and shared-link recipes are saved from the start, expiry null.

- Only Save verifies: `saveRecipeDraft` (`src/domain/recipe-import.ts`), behind MCP `save_recipe` and `POST /recipes/:id/save`, runs `verifyRecipe` and, when it passes, promotes the draft (`promoteDraft`: verified, expiry cleared, only while still `unverified`); otherwise it records and returns the problems. Saving a saved recipe is a no-op success; one discarded or swept is "Unknown recipe." (MCP error, app 404). `recheckRecipe`, `verify_recipe`, imports and edits only record problems (`recordProblems`); nothing but Save writes `verification`.
- One lock per recipe: `withRecipeLock` on the store. Save, `recheckRecipe`, `resolveUnresolvedLines` and every base or draft edit function run their check and write inside it, so no edit lands between a check and the write it allows. The live store takes the recipe's align advisory lock shared and then the recipe row (`for no key update`) in one transaction. `deleteRecipe` and `deleteDraftRecipe` take the same two (the row `for update`) before they clean up the recipe's notice dismissals or delete anything, because an edit holding the row deletes a removed ingredient's dismissals, and a delete that cleared them first would wait on the edit while the edit waits on it. An align takes the advisory lock exclusive and then the row; it already holds both, so its calls into the edit functions do not wait. The memory store keeps one holder per recipe, re-entrant within a holder.
- A saved recipe never breaks: `assertEditKeepsCheck` (`src/domain/recipes.ts`) compares the check's problem keys before and after a proposed base edit and refuses one that adds a problem (`RecipeEditBreaksCheckError`, a `RecipeInputError` listing them). Every base edit function and `alignChange` call it before writing; problems a recipe already has never block, and a draft is never refused. Reordering cannot change the check, so it is not guarded.
- Readers: the store's `listRecipes` returns saved recipes only, so every list, notice, use-soon, suggestion, search and week-plan reader leaves drafts out. `listDraftRecipes` serves `list_recipes` with `verification: "unverified"` and the Recipes page's Drafts section; `getRecipe` returns both. Planning (`planRecipe`'s `recipeIsDraft`, and the store inserts as a backstop), cooking, the grocery add, a logged portion and a share link refuse a draft with "That recipe is a draft; save it first." (`RECIPE_DRAFT_REFUSAL`).
- The draft sweep (`startDraftSweep`, wired in `src/index.ts`) runs a minute after boot and then daily: `listExpiredDrafts` finds drafts past `coalesce(draft_expires_at, created_at + 7 days)` and each goes through the store's `deleteDraftRecipe` (align lock, dismissal cleanup, and the delete only while the row is still `unverified`, so a Save that lands after the listing keeps the recipe), silently. The older unverified sweep is gone, and so is its household setting: a stored `unverifiedRecipeExpiryDays` key is ignored and dropped on the next save.
- MCP `discard_recipe_draft` (the importer, i.e. the recipe's creator, or the owner) deletes a draft through `runDestructive` and `deleteDraftRecipe` with no notification (one saved meanwhile is reported as already saved, with no undo recorded); `delete_recipe` refuses a draft and points to it. The app's draft page has Discard draft (shown to the importer and the owner), which confirms and posts to `POST /recipes/:id/discard`: the same permission, `deleteDraftRecipe`, no notification, and a draft saved since the page was drawn is kept with a 409 in place. Save shows to a viewer with `recipe.edit`. The Recipes page's Drafts section lists a member's own drafts and, for the owner, every household draft. A share link to a draft answers like an unknown token, as a page and as a document. A draft has no favourite star (the favourite route refuses one with 409), and its expiry is shown as the household's local day. A shared-link import avoids draft names as well as saved ones.
